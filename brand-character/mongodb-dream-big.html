Approach

Making People Feel Something About a Database Company

The brief was simple and enormous in equal measure: make people feel something about a database company. The answer was to stop talking about the technology and start celebrating what people do with it: the giant ideas, the world-changing applications, the developers who'd rather build something impossible than accept a reasonable constraint.
