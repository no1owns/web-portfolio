Overview

MongoDB Beliefs

Articulating the values that define MongoDB's engineering culture, developed as a visual storyboard to align leadership and express the company's soul to new employees, partners, and the broader developer community.

MongoDB Beliefs Storyboard, 2018
Approach

Values You Could Touch

Company values are usually abstract nouns. The challenge here was to make MongoDB's beliefs feel like something you could touch: specific, earned, and rooted in actual culture rather than boardroom ideals. The storyboard format let us develop a visual language for each belief before committing to final execution.
