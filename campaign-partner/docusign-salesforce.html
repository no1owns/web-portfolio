Overview

Co-Marketing at Enterprise Scale

At DocuSign I led creative for the company's most visible external partnerships — building co-branded campaign systems that had to simultaneously honor two enterprise brand identities while driving measurable adoption.

The work spanned four major technology partnerships and a parallel program of standalone DocuSign brand campaigns. Each partnership required its own visual system: shared lockups, co-branded ad suites, event materials, and digital enablement assets — all produced at the pace of a growing SaaS company pushing into enterprise accounts.

Salesforce Co-branded campaign suite driving DocuSign for Salesforce adoption across the CRM ecosystem
Google Joint go-to-market creative for DocuSign's Google Workspace integration
Microsoft Co-marketing assets for DocuSign within the Microsoft 365 and Azure partner ecosystem
Apple Campaign creative supporting DocuSign's presence in Apple business channels
Standalone Campaigns

Brand Campaigns & Digital Advertising

Alongside the partner program I led DocuSign's own brand campaign creative — including the "Just DocuSign It" and "Close Business Fast" campaign lines. These ran across paid digital, display, print, and out-of-home placements, establishing DocuSign's voice in a category it was actively defining.

The campaigns required balancing broad awareness objectives with direct-response performance metrics — producing work that read as brand-forward while still driving trial and conversion.
