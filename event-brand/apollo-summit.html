Overview

Brand Creative for a Sales Intelligence Leader

Apollo brought a distinct energy to the conference space, a company known for precision data and sales intelligence that wanted its event to feel equally sharp and confident. The creative brief called for a system that could carry the Apollo brand through a full multi-day summit: environmental signage, stage design, speaker assets, and digital promotion.

Approach

Sharp, Confident, and Built to Scale

The design system was rooted in Apollo's existing brand but pushed toward something more event-worthy: bolder type, confident use of space, and a motion language that felt at home on a large-format stage. Every asset was designed once and adapted to multiple formats: from the hero LED backdrop to the badge lanyard.
