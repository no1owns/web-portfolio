Overview

DocuSign's Biggest Annual Moment

Creative direction for DocuSign's Momentum — the company's flagship annual conference. Responsible for the end-to-end event identity: stage design, signage system, motion graphics, speaker assets, and the branded attendee experience across multiple days and tracks.

DocuSign Momentum Conference — Annual User Conference
Approach

An Experience That Builds Trust

Momentum had grown significantly and needed a visual identity that reflected DocuSign's evolution from e-signature tool to Agreement Cloud platform. The creative brief pushed for something that felt less 'software company conference' and more 'event you'd want to attend' — with energy, warmth, and a visual language that could scale from a 30-foot LED wall to a lanyard badge.
