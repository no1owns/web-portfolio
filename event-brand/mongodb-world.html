Overview

Four Years, One Growth Story

MongoDB World is the company's flagship developer conference — a multi-day gathering of thousands of engineers, architects, and database practitioners. But it was one piece of a much larger creative partnership. Across MongoDB's biggest growth years, I led creative direction spanning the conference itself, the company's NASDAQ IPO launch, an internal rebrand reveal, and a values campaign that gave the culture a visual language.

Working directly with the CEO, CTO, and CMO throughout, the throughline across every engagement stayed the same: root the creative in what MongoDB's community actually builds, not in generic enterprise polish.

MongoDB World

Built for Builders

MongoDB World brought together thousands of engineers and architects for the company's largest annual event. I owned the event identity end-to-end across three consecutive years — keynote stage design, expo presence, motion content, session graphics, and the full suite of promotional assets.

Developer audiences are notoriously hard to win over with typical event creative: glowing grids, stock server photography, and abstract data visualizations land flat with people who actually build the things those images are meant to represent. The approach instead rooted the creative in the community itself — the craft of building, the momentum of open source, "did you know" moments pulled from MongoDB's own history. Nothing about it was dressed up as a generic enterprise event in green.

IPO Launch — 2017

Taking MongoDB Public

When MongoDB prepared to go public on NASDAQ, the campaign had to translate a developer-first database platform into a story that worked for Wall Street, enterprise buyers, and the technical community all at once, without losing the credibility that made the product what it was.

Embedded with the CEO, CTO, and CMO, I built the messaging architecture around MongoDB's genuine strengths — developer velocity, enterprise scale, flexibility — then developed the visual system across OOH in Times Square and San Francisco's financial district, a national television commercial, digital advertising, and print. MongoDB priced at $24 a share and opened 34% above that on its first day of trading; the visual language became a cornerstone of the brand's evolution in the years that followed.

Dream Big Rebrand — 2018

Making People Feel Something About a Database Company

An internal rebrand reveal celebrating the ambition of the developers building on MongoDB — spanning brand film, event identity, and experiential design, unveiled to thousands of employees at MongoDB World.

The brief was simple and enormous in equal measure: make people feel something about a database company. The answer was to stop talking about the technology and start celebrating what people do with it — the giant ideas, the world-changing applications, the developers who'd rather build the impossible than accept a reasonable constraint.

Beliefs Campaign — 2018

Values You Could Touch

A companion internal campaign translating MongoDB's core engineering values into a visual language leadership could rally behind and new employees could actually feel, developed as a visual storyboard before any final execution began.

Company values are usually abstract nouns. The challenge was to make MongoDB's six core beliefs feel like something you could touch — specific, earned, and rooted in the culture that actually existed rather than boardroom ideals.
