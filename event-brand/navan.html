Overview

Visual Design for a Travel Unicorn

Navan (formerly TripActions) was redefining corporate travel at speed. The work spanned event creative, digital campaigns, and brand expression — all built to match the energy of a company moving fast and thinking big.

From event identity to sustainability campaigns, every asset was designed to feel premium, purposeful, and unmistakably Navan.

Partner Campaign

Visa × TripActions

Co-branded campaign creative for the Visa and TripActions partnership — developing a unified visual identity for a joint go-to-market push targeting corporate card and travel integration.

The work included digital campaign assets, co-branded lockups, and supporting materials bridging Visa's global brand standards with TripActions' growth-stage identity.

Partner Campaign

Lufthansa × TripActions

Partnership campaign creative connecting Lufthansa's premium airline brand with TripActions' corporate travel platform. The collaboration focused on positioning TripActions as the preferred booking layer for Lufthansa's business travel customers.
