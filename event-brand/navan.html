Overview

Visual Design for a Travel Unicorn

Navan (formerly TripActions) was redefining corporate travel at speed. The work spanned digital ad campaigns, brand expression, co-marketing partnerships, and original video production, all built to match the energy of a company moving fast and thinking big.

Every asset was designed to feel premium, purposeful, and unmistakably Navan: bold enough to stop the scroll, refined enough to hold up in enterprise sales contexts. The program extended to out-of-home, managing a $500K annual OOH budget including a co-funded campaign with Visa across key business travel markets.

Digital Campaigns

Campaign Ad Creative

A full suite of digital campaign assets, including static ads, motion executions, and social formats, built to drive awareness and acquisition across paid channels. The work stretched from broad brand awareness to performance-focused direct response, all within the TripActions visual system.

Partner Campaign

Lufthansa × TripActions

Partnership campaign creative connecting Lufthansa's premium airline brand with TripActions' corporate travel platform. The collaboration positioned TripActions as the preferred booking layer for Lufthansa's business travel customers.

Deliverables included co-branded digital assets, motion content, and a unified campaign visual system that respected both brands while driving joint acquisition goals.

Video Production

Travel & Expense Video Spot

An original video spot developed to communicate TripActions' travel and expense capabilities. The work involved creative direction of the visual narrative, motion design, and production of supporting stills that could live across digital and sales contexts.
