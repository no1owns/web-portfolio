Overview

Brand Character With Built-In Progression

Linx Security needed a visual identity that could do more than look good; it had to carry meaning through levels. The creative work spanned brand character development, moodboard exploration, and a full visual system built around four distinct stages, each with its own personality and sense of earned prestige.

Approach

Four Levels, One Coherent System

The design moved through four distinct levels, each with its own moodboard, color language, and visual personality, escalating in visual weight and detail as users advanced. Level 1 established the foundational system; Level 4 felt like an earned achievement. The visual progression was deliberate: users should feel the difference between where they started and where they ended up.
